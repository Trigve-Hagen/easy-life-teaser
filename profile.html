Phase 1: Local Data Vault Built

Documentation

Your personal vault, split across two local SQLite databases: personal_vault.db holds everyday form-filler data (name, addresses, emails, phone numbers, websites, custom fields), and personal_identity.db holds sensitive identity documents (SSN, driver's license, passport) in a separate file kept out of routine backups. Every profile can have as many addresses/emails/phone numbers as you need, each markable "primary." Everything is served through the FastAPI backend's /api/profile family of endpoints, proxied by nginx at /api/.

Document vault: the Documents page is a general filing cabinet for every filled-out form you have — organized into life-category folders (Taxes, Employment, Insurance, and more; add your own too). Every upload is scanned for text automatically, and the vault looks for facts it can offer to prefill elsewhere — an employer name, a pay amount, a hire date. Nothing gets written to your real Profile or Budget data until you review a suggestion and click Apply yourself; a document that can't be scanned (a photo with no text layer, for instance) just shows that honestly instead of guessing. The page also shows exactly where your files live on disk.

Starting from zero: if you have no profile yet, the Documents page leads with a "Get started" upload — pick any document with your basic info on it (mail, an ID, a bill) and it reads out first/last name and address for you to check over an Accept & create my profile button, editable inline before you confirm. Every upload, before or after that first profile exists, also gets an auto-suggested folder (a keyword match on the filename/text, shown before you save so you can always change it).

Finding documents already on your computer: further down the same page, you can register specific folders (like Downloads) for LifeModel to search for more documents — nothing outside a folder you've explicitly added is ever looked at, and anything under a Filesystem-page protected path is skipped and shown as blocked, never silently moved. Every found file waits in a queue for you to Import or Skip individually by default. An optional auto-import switch (off by default, with a one-time warning before you turn it on) lets it keep filing what it's confident about on its own the next time you scan — it still leaves anything low-confidence or sensitive for you to review by hand either way.

Tutorial

  1. Open the Profile page.
  2. Click + New profile, fill in your name and any basic info, then Create profile — or, on the Documents page instead, upload a document and let the "Get started" flow build your first profile from it.
  3. In the Addresses / Emails / Phone Numbers / Websites sections, click + Add, fill the small form, and save. Check "Primary" on the one you want the form-filler agent (Phase 2) to prefer.
  4. If you need SSN/license/passport stored, use the Identity Documents section — it's visibly marked "Sensitive" and lives in a separate database file.
  5. Everything saves immediately — there's no separate "save profile" step for child items.
  6. Open the Documents page. Pick a profile and a folder, then Upload & scan a document — a pay stub or W-2 works well.
  7. Click Find fillable facts on the uploaded document. Review each suggestion (what it found, how confident, and where it would go), then click Apply on the ones you want — or Dismiss the rest. Nothing is written until you click Apply.
  8. Under Find documents on this computer, add a folder (e.g. your Downloads folder) and click Scan now. Review each candidate's guessed folder before clicking Import, or Skip it.

Next steps

  • Register a real scan folder (Downloads, Desktop) so the vault starts finding documents on its own instead of one-by-one manual uploads.
  • Try the auto-import switch once you trust a few imports' folder guesses — you can always turn it back off from the same panel.
  • Once a document's in, click Find fillable facts and apply anything useful to your Profile or Budget (Phase 8).
