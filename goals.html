Phase 17: Marketable Skills, Goals & Reward Agent — Preview

Documentation

The point of this phase is happiness, not just productivity — everything on the Goals page gets checked against what you actually said would make you happy, and hitting a goal earns a real, self-defined reward, not just a status flip.

Life values: the guided intake asks three fixed prompts (what you enjoy even when it's hard, what you'd do if money didn't matter, what drains you) and stores each answer as its own structured, categorized row — never one freeform blob — so the opportunity agent below can actually query it. You can also add values by hand at any time, each with a category (interest/strength/value/dealbreaker/energizer/drainer) and an importance rating.

Skills & skill gaps: track what you already have, at what proficiency, and whether it's marketable and enjoyable. A skill gap records something worth learning — often because it kept coming up in researched opportunities — with a status you move from identified through learning to acquired.

Goals & milestones: a goal is a real plan, not a wish — break it into milestones and the progress bar tracks real completion. A goal can only move to completed once every milestone is done; trying to complete one early is rejected with an explicit "not every milestone is done yet" error unless you deliberately override it, and that override is logged.

Opportunity research agent: click Research more opportunities and opportunity_agent.py reads your real life values and marketable (advanced/expert-proficiency) skills, then runs public web search (reusing the same DuckDuckGo-HTML search Phase 15's competitor research already uses — no login-gated content, no bypassing a site's terms) to draft ideas. Every drafted idea carries a real source link and a note on which values/skills it matched — nothing is invented to fill a quota, and a profile with no life values or skills recorded yet gets an honest empty result instead of a fabricated one. Every idea starts at suggested; moving one further, or turning it into a goal, is always your own manual step.

Rewards: you define your own rewards up front — same self-defined-values spirit as life values. Link one to a goal and it flips from planned to earned automatically the instant that goal completes, surfaced right at the top of the page so it's the first thing you see. Claiming a reward is always a separate, explicit click — the system never "spends" it on your behalf. If a reward has an estimated cost and Phase 8's budget is set up, Check against budget gives an honest advisory note against this month's real projected surplus — never a block.

Tutorial

  1. Open the Goals page and pick a profile.
  2. Under Life values, click Guided intake and answer at least one prompt honestly.
  3. Under Marketable skills, add a skill you're actually good at — mark its proficiency and whether it's marketable.
  4. Under Goals, add a goal and a couple of milestones, then try marking it completed before the milestones are done — check that it's rejected until you finish them (or explicitly override).
  5. Add a Reward linked to that goal before you finish it, then mark the remaining milestones done and complete the goal — watch the reward flip to earned at the top of the page, then claim it.
  6. Under Opportunity ideas, click Research more opportunities and check that every idea returned has a real source link and an honest note on which value/skill it matched.
