Phase 11: Company Registry & Marketing Plan Agent Built

Documentation

Adds a Company registry — a company is owned either by a profile you control (employee_owned) or by a real Phase 9 Employer (employer, optionally linked to a Phase 10 Business for real financials). Each company gets one or more named marketing plans made of MarketingPlanElement rows — target-audience notes, messaging, one row per channel, an optional budget line, and KPI targets — plus a MarketingResult log for recording real metrics against any element later. Every element you create lands at status="draft"; nothing is ever auto-published or auto-spent.

Steps 32–35/55 add employer-side workforce coordination on top of the core plan tooling: Work assignments can only go to a Phase 9 participant who is already opted_in on their profit-share proposal — assigning work only requests tracking, it never grants it, and only the assigned person can move tracking_consent to opted_in. Once consented, progress syncs through progress_tracker.py or the dashboard directly, gated on both that consent and a matching, active, opaque WorkerDevice registration — never keystroke logs, screenshots, screen recording, browser history, or file-content scraping, regardless of what an employer might ask for. Revoking a device makes its very next sync fail closed. The performance monitor gives the employer a read-only rollup (total time logged, latest task-completion percentage, optional per-device breakdown) — visibility only, never an automated action — and every load is written to the linked business's access audit log so a participant can always see who looked, and when. Territories describe a marketing target (a place or demographic, never a location a device must physically sit in) with minimum hardware requirements; a TerritoryContract only moves to accepted once the linked device's real specs are checked against those minimums — reject, never a silent pass.

Tutorial

  1. Open the Marketing page and pick a profile.
  2. Create a company — Employee-owned, or Employer with a real Employer ID and (optionally) a linked Business ID.
  3. Review its elements — each has an editable status (draft → proposed → active → paused/completed) you move by hand as the plan actually happens.
  4. On an employer company, open Work assignments and assign work to an opted-in participant. Open the assignment, move tracking_consent to opted_in, register a device, and log a progress metric — notice logging is blocked until both are true.
  5. Click Load performance monitor to see the rollup, then check the linked business's Access Audit Log on the Business Stats page — your read just showed up there.
  6. Add a Territory with minimum hardware requirements, propose a contract with a weak device and watch it get rejected on Accept, then propose one with a device that actually qualifies.

Next steps

  • Add your real employer's company here.
  • Assign work to an opted-in colleague and watch the performance monitor roll up their real progress.
  • Try a territory contract with a device that doesn't meet the minimums, and confirm it's rejected rather than silently accepted.
  • Watch Phase 15 (Business Model) once built — it extends this same Company registry with financial statements, invoices, and proposals.
