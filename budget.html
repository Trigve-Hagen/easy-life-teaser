Phase 8: Budget & Finance Tracking — Preview

Documentation

Tracks checking/savings accounts, credit cards, and loans (all monetary values stored as exact decimals, never floating point), bills with recurrence, and income sources. finance_utils.py runs real amortization math — month-by-month interest/principal/balance — to project when a card or loan pays off and how much interest that costs, and lets you compare "what if I paid $X more/month" against the current plan. The budget agent answers a handful of specific question patterns (afford-a-purchase, which-card-first, this-month-summary, upcoming-bills) by querying that same real data — every answer states the numbers it used, and an unrecognized question says so instead of guessing.

Avalanche (highest-APR-first) vs. snowball (smallest-balance-first) payoff ordering is a suggestion the agent computes, never something it acts on — you decide which debt to attack first.

Every loan also gets a strategy toolkit: upload the actual signed documents (PDF text is extracted automatically — a scanned/image-only PDF won't extract text yet, that's a known gap, not silently faked), research the lender's own practices and complaint history plus refinance/balance-transfer/assistance-program options via real, cited web search, and run an analysis that combines your loan's real numbers, the uploaded documents (it specifically checks for prepayment-penalty language before suggesting extra payments), and that research into concrete suggestions ranked against a payoff goal you set (minimize interest, minimize payment, payoff ASAP, or eliminate via negotiation/forgiveness). Every suggestion cites what it used — either the exact math or a source URL — and nothing more than a status label ("interested"/"pursuing"/"dismissed") ever changes on its own; contacting a lender or accepting an offer is entirely up to you. None of this is legal or financial advice.

Tutorial

  1. Open the Budget page and pick a profile.
  2. Add an account — for a credit card or loan, the form expands to ask for APR/limit/payment (credit card) or principal/rate/payment (loan).
  3. Click Payoff projection on any credit card or loan to see months-to-payoff and total interest at the current payment, plus a "what if I pay this much/month" comparison.
  4. On a loan, click Loan strategy tools: set a payoff goal, upload any signed loan documents, click Research lender (enter the lender's name) and Research funding sources, then Analyze strategies to draft cited suggestions. Mark any you want to act on as "Interested" or "Pursuing" — that's a label for you, not an action taken on your behalf.
  5. Add bills (with either a day-of-month for recurring monthly bills, or an explicit next due date for other recurrences) and income sources — the This month panel at the top updates to show projected surplus or deficit.
  6. Try the Ask the budget agent box — e.g. "can I afford a $200 purchase?" — the answer always shows its work.
