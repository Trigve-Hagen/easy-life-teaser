Phase 10: Employer Business Info & Stats Sharing Built

Documentation

Lets a business owner record real per-period business stats (revenue, profit, growth rate, employee count) behind a Phase 9 profit-share proposal, and share only what they choose with specific employees. Sharing is per-field, not per-row: each recorded stat period has its own list of which fields are shareable — an owner might mark profit and growth_rate shareable while keeping itemized revenue and employee_count internal-only. Fields not marked shareable are never returned to a summary_only grant, no exceptions.

Access is default-deny and explicit: an employee sees nothing until the owner creates an access grant naming them, with an access level of summary_only (shareable fields only) or detailed (everything). A grant is never created automatically just because someone opted into a profit-share proposal — it's always a separate owner action. Revoking a grant takes effect immediately: the very next read using that grant's ID is rejected, nothing is cached.

Read the security model honestly: this app has no login system anywhere and dashboard.site stays bound to this machine only. A grant's ID is the closest thing to a credential an employee presents — it's revocable and every gated read is written to the access audit log below, but it is not cryptographic proof of who's actually asking. The real security boundary is still who has physical or network access to this computer, exactly like every other page in this vault.

The shared progress view combines the latest permitted stats with the linked Phase 9 proposal's live projection — profit over projection, pool amount, and estimated per-employee share — computed by the exact same math the Employer page itself uses, so the two views can never silently drift apart.

Tutorial

  1. Open the Business Stats page and click + Add business to record the business's legal name and industry.
  2. On the Employer page, edit the matching employer record and set its business_id to this business's ID, so a proposal can pull real stats.
  3. Back on Business Stats, select the business and click + Add stat period — enter the real revenue/profit/growth/headcount figures, and check only the fields you want a summary_only employee to ever see.
  4. Click + Grant access, name the employee, and pick Summary only or Detailed. Note the grant's ID shown in the list — you'll hand this and the business's ID (#shown above the grants list) to the employee directly.
  5. Scroll to Shared progress view at the bottom of the page (or have the employee do this themselves) and enter the Business ID and Grant ID to see exactly what that employee sees.
  6. Try revoking the grant, then submit the shared progress view again with the same IDs — confirm it's rejected immediately.
  7. Check the Access audit log on the business detail view — every successful employee read is listed there with who and when.

Next steps
